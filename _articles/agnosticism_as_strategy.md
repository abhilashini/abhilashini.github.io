title: "Agnosticism as Strategy"
---
Being "agnostic" is frequently misinterpreted as a refusal to commit to a specific tool. In reality, it is a commitment to the problem rather than the vendor. When we design agnostically, we are building a buffer between our business logic and the shifting sands of the infrastructure market.

It is an exercise in future-proofing. By defining clear interfaces, we grant our future selves the permission to change our minds without having to rewrite the world. 

The cost of this abstraction is high at the start, but the cost of being locked into a dying paradigm is infinitely higher.
