# COLOPHON

### DESIGN LINEAGE
This publication is a spatial adaptation of **Neoplasticism**. The visual logic—characterized by a primary color palette and non-symmetric orthogonal grids—is a direct homage to the **De Stijl** movement and the theories of **Piet Mondrian**.

***

### TECHNICAL STACK
Built with architectural resilience using **Jekyll** and **GitHub Pages**. Diagrams are rendered via **Mermaid.js**, custom-themed to maintain aesthetic isomorphism with the grid.
